## 4. Data-quality findings

### DQ-001 (HIGH) - owner, persistency

**Columns:** `AppID`

One AppID is stored in Excel scientific notation '1.50E+10' in Owner_Details and Persistency_Details. It is the only 8-character AppID in either file; every other AppID is 11 characters.

- *Impact:* Breaks the Policy-Owner and Policy-Persistency join for exactly one policy.
- *Handling:* NOT silently repaired. The loader exposes it as an explicit, logged repair rule (config: repair_scientific_notation_appid). When disabled, the row is reported as an orphan rather than guessed.

### DQ-002 (HIGH) - policy

**Columns:** `Login.Date`, `Inforce.Date`

Both Policy date columns mix separators ('/' and '-') within the same column. All values are month-first; the dash-separated subset is ambiguous on its own and was resolved by cross-validation.

- *Impact:* Naive single-format parsing silently drops ~17-33% of rows.
- *Handling:* Contract prescribes the ordered format list ['%m/%d/%Y', '%m-%d-%Y'].

### DQ-003 (MEDIUM) - claims

**Columns:** `Claim.Amount`, `Amount.Paid`

Monetary values are stored as text with thousands separators, including Indian lakh grouping (e.g. '12,50,000.00', '1,66,500').

- *Impact:* float() on the raw string raises; naive coercion yields nulls.
- *Handling:* Contract prescribes comma stripping before numeric coercion.

### DQ-004 (MEDIUM) - claims

**Columns:** `Date.of.Intimation`, `Settlement.Date`, `Status.of.Claim`, `Amount.Paid`

Claim date columns are predominantly '%d-%b-%y' but contain a small number of '%m/%d/%Y' values. The single unsettled claim carries the literal string 'WIP' in three columns at once - Status.of.Claim, Amount.Paid and Settlement.Date - so 'WIP' is a sentinel in a monetary and a date column, not only in the status column.

- *Impact:* Settlement-lag and amount-paid metrics must exclude the unsettled claim rather than coerce 'WIP' to zero or null silently.
- *Handling:* 'WIP' is a declared sentinel meaning not-yet-settled. It is treated as missing for date and money maths, and the claim is excluded from settlement-lag and payout-ratio metrics with the exclusion reported.

### DQ-005 (MEDIUM) - sales

**Columns:** `First Year Persistency Rate`

First Year Persistency Rate is null for a material share of agents.

- *Impact:* Agent persistency rankings must declare the eligible population.
- *Handling:* Rankings exclude null-rate agents and report the excluded count.

### DQ-006 (MEDIUM) - policy, persistency

**Columns:** `POLICY.STATUS`

Persistency_Details contains only Premium paying (regular) and Lapsed policies. Surrendered and Death policies have no persistency row.

- *Impact:* Persistency metrics computed over Policy_Details would use a wrong denominator.
- *Handling:* Persistency PSs declare the eligible population explicitly.

### DQ-007 (LOW) - policy, claims

**Columns:** `AppID`

Claims and Persistency are 1:1 with Policy in this dataset, not 1:N as the documented relationship allows. At most one claim row exists per policy.

- *Impact:* Joins cannot fan out, but code must not hard-code the 1:1 assumption.
- *Handling:* Joins remain many-safe; row-count assertions guard against silent fan-out.

### DQ-008 (LOW) - policy

**Columns:** `CHANNEL`, `Mode`

CHANNEL is 'Banca' for all 25,000 rows and Mode is 'Annually' for all rows.

- *Impact:* Channel and payment-mode comparison questions cannot be answered.
- *Handling:* No PS may offer channel or mode comparison; the limitation is stated.

### DQ-009 (LOW) - owner

**Columns:** `Owner.Age`, `Owner.Earned.Income`, `Owner.Comm.Postal.Code`, `Owner.Gender`

Owner attributes contain implausible values: ages as low as 1, an earned income up to 8,384,891,049, postal codes as low as 1, and a third gender code 'C' alongside M/F.

- *Impact:* Means are distorted by extreme values; demographic PSs need medians or caveats.
- *Handling:* Distribution PSs report medians alongside means and flag outliers.

### DQ-010 (LOW) - policy

**Columns:** `Inforce.month`

Inforce.month is an unpadded YYYYM/YYYYMM integer: '20231' means 2023-01 while '202310' means 2023-10. Lexical or numeric sorting is wrong.

- *Impact:* Trend ordering breaks unless the field is decomposed into year and month.
- *Handling:* Contract prescribes deriving period from Inforce.Date, not this column.

### DQ-011 (LOW) - policy

**Columns:** `Login.Date`, `Inforce.Date`

A policy has a Login.Date later than its Inforce.Date.

- *Impact:* Underwriting-turnaround metrics can produce a negative duration.
- *Handling:* Turnaround PSs report and exclude negative durations.

### DQ-012 (MEDIUM) - owner

**Columns:** `Owner.Comm.Postal.Code`

Owner.Comm.Postal.Code is not numeric. Two records hold alphanumeric foreign postal codes, matching owners with a non-India address.

- *Impact:* Coercing the column to a number silently discards these two values and would strip leading zeros from any code that has one.
- *Handling:* The column is typed as an identifier and kept as text. Identifier columns are never summed or averaged, and join keys compare exactly rather than through floating-point equality.

### DQ-013 (LOW) - policy, owner, sales, claims, persistency

**Columns:** `claims.AppID`, `claims.Claim_No`, `owner.AppID`, `owner.ClientID`, `owner.Owner.Comm.Postal.Code`, `persistency.AppID`, `persistency.SALES_ID`, `policy.AppID`, `policy.Branch_No`, `policy.Inforce.month`, `policy.SALES_ID`, `sales.SALES_ID`

Twelve columns are numeric-looking identifiers or codes rather than quantities: AppID, ClientID, SALES_ID, Branch_No, Claim_No, Inforce.month and Owner.Comm.Postal.Code.

- *Impact:* Typed as numbers they would permit meaningless arithmetic such as an average AppID, and would make every join depend on float equality.
- *Handling:* All are typed as identifiers and held as text throughout the system.


## 5. Domains the data cannot support

These are excluded from the Problem Statement catalogue by construction.

| Domain | Reason |
|---|---|
| channel comparison | CHANNEL has a single value ('Banca') across all 25,000 policies. |
| payment-mode comparison at policy level | Policy.Mode has a single value ('Annually'). Persistency.Payment_Mode does vary. |
| premium collection / payment transactions | No transaction-level premium table exists in the five CSVs. |
| agent demographics, tenure, hierarchy or geography | Sales_Details holds only performance rates and an Active/Inactive Type flag. |
| policyholder health, medical underwriting or risk scoring | No underwriting, medical or risk-score columns exist. |
| commission, cost, profitability or expense analysis | No commission or expense columns exist in any of the five files. |
| fund performance / NAV analysis for ULIP policies | No fund, NAV or unit columns exist despite ULIP policies being present. |
| customer contact, servicing or complaint case history | Only an aggregate agent-level complaint rate exists, with no case records. |
